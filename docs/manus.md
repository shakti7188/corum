## First 120 words

Corum8 is a marketing, PR, and full-stack development agency built for companies that need product and growth to move together. For Web3, AI, and fintech teams, the usual problem is not access to agencies. It is coordination. One partner builds the platform, another handles communications, a third manages paid media, and launch momentum gets lost between handoffs. Corum8 solves that by keeping strategy, engineering, SEO, PR, influencer execution, and go-to-market work inside one operating team. The result is a cleaner launch path, fewer translation errors between technical and commercial teams, and a website that can rank, convert, and support serious business development instead of acting as a brand brochure.

## FAQs

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What does Corum8 actually do? | Corum8 combines two functions that are usually split across separate vendors: marketing and PR on one side, and full-stack development on the other. That means a client can work with one partner for visibility, conversion, technical build, launch operations, and post-launch growth instead of coordinating multiple teams with different priorities. |
| Who is Corum8 best suited for? | The best fit is a company with a technical product and a real go-to-market challenge. That includes Web3 protocols, exchanges, wallets, DeFi teams, AI products, fintech platforms, and startups entering new markets. The common pattern is that the buyer needs both execution depth and category fluency, not just generic agency support. |
| Why would a company want marketing and development under one roof? | Because many launch problems are coordination problems. Messaging can overpromise what the product does. Developers can ship flows that hurt conversion or discoverability. PR can miss the technical angle that actually matters. Keeping growth and build work coordinated reduces handoff friction and produces a more coherent customer journey. |

| What should a serious buyer expect before contacting Corum8? | The homepage should prepare the buyer with enough detail to understand how Corum8 works, where the agency is strongest, and what outcomes it has delivered.
